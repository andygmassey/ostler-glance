# Demo video script (target: under 3 minutes)

Recorded on the Even Hub simulator against the fictional world in
FICTIONAL_WORLD.md. Voice-over + on-screen captions. No latency numbers
anywhere; the promise is "by the time they've finished their sentence".

## Cast (all fictional)

Henry Ashworth (wearer, independent consultant) · Eleanor Whitfield
(Events Director, Halcyon Summit) · James Hardwick (Co-founder, Ledger &
Loom) · Rachel Fenwick (Principal, Cormorant Capital).

## Beats

**0:00–0:20 — The problem.**
Voice-over: "You meet dozens of people. They say their name… and by the
time the handshake ends, it's gone. Notes apps don't help — you're
staring at your phone instead of at them."
Caption: "Names are everyone's nemesis."

**0:20–0:55 — The catch (hero).**
Screen: the recall card for the next event. Henry taps "start
transcribing". A meeting begins (Eleanor clip plays). On screen:
"Transcribing…" then "Caught name: Eleanor Whitfield · Halcyon Summit".
Voice-over: "Ostler Glance catches the name while they're still
introducing themselves — and holds it on screen. You never break eye
contact."
Caption: "Caught before the sentence ends."

**0:55–1:40 — The memory.**
Screen: tap to end the capture; "Capture ended — farmed." Cut to: the next morning, card
for "Halcyon Summit — speaker check-in — Monday 30 November". Scroll to
the promises ledger.
Voice-over: "When the meeting ends, Ostler farms the record — what was
said, what was agreed, who owes what. The next time you're about to walk
into a room with someone, the card is already there. What you promised
them. What they promised you. And how long ago."
On screen: "→ you owe: Talk abstract (23d)".

**1:40–2:20 — The act.**
Screen: one tap — "Send abstract email" — confirm state "Done".
Voice-over: "And it closes the loop in one tap. The follow-up you
promised, drafted from the record, sent from your own account. Not a
reminder to do it later — done."
Caption: "Even AI answers when you ask. Ostler Glance tells you before
you walk in."

**2:20–3:00 — Privacy + ownership.**
Voice-over: "None of this needs a cloud. Speech-to-text and the
extraction model run on your own machine. Audio is transcribed and
deleted — nothing is kept. Your notes live in files you control. Capture
only starts when you tap, and the glasses show you whenever they're
listening."
Caption: "Nothing leaves the machine. Audio is transcribed and deleted.
Tap-to-start only. No voice prints — ever."

**3:00 — End card.**
"Ostler Glance — never forget a name." + repo link.
